Executive Summary & System Scope

LaundryLab is modernizing its multi-location laundromat business by replacing manual spreadsheets with a unified, 100% cashless digital web application. The platform eliminates cash-handling risks, automates pricing calculations using LaundryLab's official rate list, prevents garment loss through strict deposit/return matching, and provides ownership with real-time multi-branch financial visibility.

Zero Hardware Retrofit: No expensive IoT or electrical modifications are required inside washing machines. All transactions originate at the counter, and staff manage machine operations manually while the web application handles pricing, payments, receipts, SMS links, and reconciliation.

The Three System Perspectives

Perspective 1: The Business Owner
Executive Control & Oversight
  • Multi-Branch Real-Time Overview: Live side-by-side view of daily revenue, load counts, and throughput across all ground-floor branches.
  • Cashless Revenue Split: Automated separation of Speed Point POS Card Receipts, Online Mobile Payments, and LaundryPass Accounts.
  • Garment Loss Prevention Audit: Real-time reconciliation ensuring 100% of items deposited at intake match items handed back upon customer pickup across all locations.
  • Staff Shift Accountability: Audit log tracking which staff member logged each transaction and reconciled shift totals.
  • 1-Click Excel CSV Export: Instant download of complete financial and transaction ledgers formatted for tax and accounting software.
Perspective 2: The Branch Staff Member
Branch Counter Station
  • Multi-User Staff Authentication (RBAC): Individual username/PIN login per branch (Mon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM) with audit stamping.
  • Official LaundryLab Price Calculator: Instant calculation of scale weight (kg) or pair count against official rate rules (R25/kg to R80/pair).
  • Mandatory Item Counting & SMS Dispatch: Capture sub-counts (Shirts, Pants, Jackets, Towels) and trigger automated SMS Digital Receipts to customer smartphones.
  • Garment Return Matching Checklist: Verify returned items against original deposit records to guarantee a 100% match before handover.
  • 7:00 PM Shift Reconciliation: Balance physical Speed Point card slips against system records at shift closing.
Perspective 3: The Customer
SMS Mobile Links & Live Tracker
  • SMS Receipt Dispatch: Customer receives an instant SMS containing their digital receipt upon counter deposit.
  • Online Payment Web Link: Customer taps link in SMS to pay via Apple Pay, Google Pay, Credit/Debit Card, or Instant EFT.
  • Live Load Countdown Tracker Link: Customer taps link to view a live cycle countdown timer (e.g. 28:14 remaining) on their smartphone.
  • Automated Readiness Alert: SMS text notification sent when laundry is washed, folded/ironed, and ready for pickup at the counter.
